## Finding products

The range is in the sidebar, ordered by category; category pages filter and sort by price and properties. **Search** finds products by name, description or article number, also by parts of a word.

## Into the basket

On the product page you choose a variant if there are several (e.g. a length) and the quantity. Some goods can be ordered in decimal quantities, e.g. 2.5 m³. Larger quantities often get cheaper graduated prices; they are listed on the product page.

**Made-to-measure products** you put together yourself: choose options, enter dimensions in millimetres, and the price is calculated at once, line by line. Invalid dimensions are shown right at the field.

**Add to basket** adds the product. The basket (top right) always shows current prices; there you change quantities or **Remove** lines. You can fill your basket without an account: when you sign in later, it is kept.

## Checkout

**Proceed to checkout** takes you to sign in if you are not signed in yet (an account is quickly made, see *Your account*). At checkout you choose:

1. **Billing address** and **delivery address** from your addresses, or you add new ones.
2. **Shipping:** parcel, freight (for large and heavy goods) or pickup. Which are possible depends on the goods; the costs are shown with them.
3. **Payment:** see *Paying*.
4. Optionally a **note** about the order.

On the right are the totals with VAT. Ticking the terms and **Place binding order** makes the order binding.

## After ordering

You get an order confirmation by email, with the confirmation as PDF. **My account → My orders** lists all your orders with their state:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Placed | your order has arrived |
| Confirmed | we have checked and accepted it |
| Paid | your payment has arrived |
| Shipped / Ready for pickup | on its way to you, or ready to be collected |
| Completed | delivered or collected |

We let you know about the important steps (confirmation, payment, shipping or ready for pickup, cancellation) by email. To change or cancel something, write to us through **Advice** or by email.
