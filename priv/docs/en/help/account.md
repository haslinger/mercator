## Registering and signing in

**Register** asks for your name, email address and a password; you get an email with a link to confirm it.

You can sign in in two ways:

- with email address and **password**,
- without a password: **Request magic link** sends you a link that signs you in. If there is no account for the address yet, it is created; we then ask for your name.

With **Remember me** you stay signed in on this device for 30 days, even when you close the browser or the installed app. Better leave it unticked on shared devices. **Sign out** ends it.

**Forgot your password?** sends a link to set a new one. After several wrong attempts, signing in is blocked for a quarter of an hour; that protects your account.

## My account

The person icon at the top right leads to:

- **My account:** name, phone and language (German or English; our emails to you use it too), and **Change password**. Changing the password signs you out everywhere.
- **My addresses:** as many addresses as you like, with company and VAT number for business customers. One each is your default billing and default delivery address; checkout preselects them. We can only deliver to the countries offered for delivery addresses.
- **My orders:** all orders with their state, items and order confirmation (PDF).
- **My offers:** offers from our advisers, see *Advice and offers*.
- **Sign out.**
