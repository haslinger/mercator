## Advice in the chat

At the bottom right of every page, **Advice** opens the chat with our experts; on product pages **Get advice** starts a conversation about that very product. You don't need an account. The chat stays open while you browse the shop.

- **If nobody is available right now**, a two-minute countdown runs. Then you can ask for an **answer by email** or a **callback**; we will get back to you.
- **In the conversation** we can recommend products (as a card with image, price and a link to the product), and send links to useful pages and files such as plans or data sheets.
- **If you have left the shop**, we send our answers by email, with a link back into the chat (for guests only if you gave us an email address).
- **After the conversation** we are glad about a rating with 1 to 5 stars; **No, thanks** skips it.

If you sign in during a conversation, it belongs to your account afterwards.

## Offers

For larger projects we make you an offer, also with items such as assembly or delivery. You need an account for it.

- You find it under **My account → My offers**, as a card in the chat and in an email with the offer as PDF.
- The offer is valid until the date it states. If we change it, you get a new version with the same number.
- **Accept offer** leads to checkout: you choose addresses, shipping and payment as usual, and the items and prices come unchanged from the offer. Your basket stays untouched.
- **Print** and **Open PDF** show the offer as a document.

An expired offer can no longer be ordered; just ask us for a new one.
