At checkout you choose how to pay. Which methods are offered depends on the shop, the shipping method and your account.

| Method | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Pay online** | After ordering you go straight to our payment provider's page, e.g. for card or EPS transfer. Then you come back to your order; it is paid as soon as the payment is confirmed. |
| **Prepayment** | You transfer the amount stating the order number; the account details are in the order confirmation and on the order page. |
| **Invoice** | You get an invoice with the delivery, payable within 14 days. Only for customers we have approved for it. |
| **Payment on pickup** | in cash or by card when you collect the goods; for pickup only. |

## Paying online

- **If the payment failed** or you cancelled it, your order stays and the goods remain reserved. The order page (**My account → My orders**) then offers **Pay now** for a new attempt.
- **When the payment has arrived**, you get an email "Payment received".
- Paying online is only possible when the amount is final; for freight goods whose costs are still open you pay differently.

## Cancellation and refund

If an order already paid online is cancelled, we refund the amount through the payment provider to the same payment method; the cancellation email tells you so. If you paid another way, we refund an amount already paid as well.
