**Administration → Payment providers** is where you choose who customers pay through with **Pay online**, or **No online payment**.

Which providers are available is decided by whoever operates the shop; the credentials (such as the Mollie key) are in its configuration, not here. Without them a provider shows **Not set up**. Payments already started keep their provider.

In test mode (Mollie with a test key, or the demo's simulator) no real money moves.
