**Catalogue → Categories** shows the range as a tree.

- **Sorting and moving:** grab a category by its handle ☰ and drag it within its level, into another category or to the top level. A category cannot be moved below itself.
- **New:** **New category** for the top level, **+** for a subcategory.
- **Editing:** name and description in German and English, the address in the shop (made from the name) and the **text below the products** (Markdown), shown on the category page below the product list.
- **State:** **Activate** (visible in the shop), **Hide** (hidden in the shop) or back to draft.
- **Deleting:** only empty categories, without subcategories or products.

Each entry shows its state and number of products. The top categories appear in the shop's sidebar under "Products".
