**Content → Terms and conditions** is where you maintain the general terms. Every order remembers the version that applied when it was placed, so the terms are versioned.

- **The list** shows all versions with their status: **in force**, **upcoming** or replaced.
- **New version:** starts with the text in force (Markdown) and needs a date from which it applies: today or later.
- **Upcoming versions** can be changed or deleted until their date. Versions that apply or applied stay unchanged.

The shop always shows the newest version whose date has come (the "Terms" link in the footer and at checkout).
