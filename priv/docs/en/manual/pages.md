**Content → Pages** is where you build the shop's pages: start page, about us, FAQ, contact, imprint and everything else.

## The page tree

Pages form a tree, and their address follows it, e.g. `/pages/contact/directions`. Dragging sorts and moves pages. When a page is moved or renamed, its old addresses keep forwarding to the new one. Subpages appear on their parent page as cards.

**New page** creates a page, **+** a subpage. Only drafts can be deleted; published pages are archived.

## Content from blocks

A page consists of blocks, which you choose with **Add block**, edit one at a time and arrange by dragging: text (Markdown), hero image, text with image, image, gallery, download, products, categories, call to action, columns, FAQ, contact form and shared block.

**Shared blocks** (their own list under Pages) are maintained once and shown on several pages, e.g. opening hours. A shared block always shows its published version.

## Working copy and publishing

Everything you change goes into the working copy first; visitors keep seeing the published version.

- **Preview:** the working copy in a new tab, as it will look.
- **Publish:** the working copy goes online. Every published version stays in the **History**; **Restore** brings an older one back into the working copy.
- **Discard changes:** the working copy becomes the published version again.
- **Hide:** the page stays reachable by its link but appears in no menu. **Archive** takes it offline.

## Settings

Title, address, menu (none, **Main navigation** in the sidebar, or **Footer**), **Icon in the navigation** and the description for search engines. **Use as start page** makes a published page the shop's start page.
