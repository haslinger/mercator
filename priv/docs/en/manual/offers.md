Offers usually come out of a conversation, but can also be created directly under **Sales → Offers**. An offer needs a customer account.

## Creating an offer

- **From the sales desk:** **New offer** in the *Offers* box next to the conversation. The offer then belongs to the conversation and appears there as a card once it is sent.
- **From the list:** **New offer**, then search for the customer (name or email).

## Editing

While the offer is a draft, every change is saved at once.

- **Items:** products from the search (with their default configuration), **Take over basket** (the customer's current basket, made-to-measure items from the configurator included) and free-text items such as "Assembly".
- **Per item:** quantity, unit price (the list price stays visible), discount and delivery time.
- **For the offer:** validity (30 days suggested), an overall discount and a note for the customer. The totals with VAT are below.

## Sending and approval

If an item ends up more than 10 % below its list price (unit price, item and overall discount together), the offer needs approval: instead of **Send** the button reads **Submit for approval**. Sales managers get an email and can **Approve and send** or return the offer with a reason. Offers made by sales managers and administrators need no approval.

Once sent, the customer gets an email with the offer as PDF and finds it under **My offers**; ordering it goes through the normal checkout, at exactly the offer's prices.

## Afterwards

- **Revise:** a new version with the same number (e.g. `…/2`) that replaces the old one; for sent and expired offers.
- **Withdraw:** the offer no longer applies.
- **Copy:** a new offer with a new number for the same customer, at today's list prices (prices set by hand are kept). Useful for repeat orders or expired offers.
- **Print** and **Open PDF:** the offer as a document.

The shop marks expired offers itself every night.
