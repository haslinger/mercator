**Administration → Users** shows administrators all accounts with their roles.

| Role | May |
|---|---|
| Administration | everything, including users, tax classes and payment providers |
| Sales | advice, offers, orders, contact requests, incidents |
| Sales management | like sales, plus approving offers with higher discounts |
| Content management | pages, media, terms, contact requests |
| Product management | products and categories |

Accounts without a role are customers. One person can have several roles.

Editing an account sets its roles and **May pay by invoice**: only approved customers see the payment method invoice. You cannot take away your own administrator role.
