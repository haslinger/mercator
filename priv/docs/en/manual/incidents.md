Incidents are problems someone has to look after. The number of open ones is next to **Incidents** in the sidebar.

## Where they come from

- **From the shop itself:** a PDF could not be created, an email not delivered, a payment does not match its order, a refund was refused, too many sign-in attempts from one address. When the same problem happens again, the open incident counts up instead of creating new ones.
- **From you:** **Report incident** on an order, an offer or at the sales desk creates an incident linked to it; **New incident** in the list for anything else, e.g. a complaint on the phone.

## Working on them

The list shows open incidents first and filters by state and kind. An incident shows what happened, what it concerns (with links) and the technical details for whoever fixes the cause. **Resolve** asks for a short description of the solution; **Reopen** brings an incident back.

Administrators see all incidents; sales staff those about customers, orders, offers and conversations, and the ones they reported.
