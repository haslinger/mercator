Messages from the contact form on a page arrive under **Sales → Contact requests** and are also emailed to the shop address; **Reply** in your email program answers the sender directly.

The list shows either the **open** or **all** requests, newest first, with the sender, the page the request came from and the message. **Mark as handled** removes a request from the open ones; **Reopen** brings it back.

Against spam: forms filled in by a bot are silently dropped, and at most five messages per hour are accepted from one address.
