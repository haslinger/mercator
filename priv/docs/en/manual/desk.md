Customers start a conversation with the **Advice** button at the bottom right of the shop, or with **Get advice** on a product page. You answer them under **Sales → Sales desk**.

## Being available

The **Available / Away** switch shows whether you take conversations. While you are available and the desk is open, new conversations play a tone and show a browser notification. Your choice survives a reload; a new tab starts as "Away". After signing in, the desk opens and you are available at once. On your own phone or in the installed app, sign in with **Remember me**; you then stay signed in for 30 days, even when the phone closes the app.

While someone is waiting, a **blinking red light** at the top shows how many are waiting and for how long; **Open** goes to the first conversation. The number is also in the browser tab's title, e.g. "(2) Sales desk", so you notice it from another tab.

If nobody is available, the customer sees a three-minute countdown and can then ask for an answer by email or a phone callback. These conversations are marked **Callback** or **Reply by email** in the list, and the shop also emails its own address.

## Notifications on your phone

With **Notify this device** at the top of the desk, the shop tells you about new conversations even when the desk is closed: on your phone even with Chrome closed, on your computer as long as the browser is running (also in the background). You get a notification

- when someone starts a **new conversation** (everyone with notifications on, at the same time), and
- when the customer writes in **your conversation**, unless you have it open at the desk.

The notification shows the name and the start of the message and usually arrives after about **three seconds** (measured on the demo with an Android phone). On Android it also arrives when an app like Hibernator has stopped Chrome (tested). Tapping it opens the conversation. To switch it on:

1. Open the shop on the device (on a phone in **Chrome**), sign in and go to the desk.
2. Tap **Notify this device** and allow notifications. The button turns green: **Notifications on**.
3. **Test** sends you a sample.
4. On Android, exempt Chrome from **battery optimisation** (Settings → Apps → Chrome → Battery → "Unrestricted"). Otherwise the phone may delay notifications.
5. On Android the **first notification may be flagged as possible spam**: Android hides it and asks whether you want to see it anyway. Show it, then confirm that it is **not spam**. After that, notifications arrive normally.

**Shop installed as an app?** If you installed the shop as an app on your Android phone (e.g. "Biber Holz" on the home screen), its notifications belong to that app, no longer to Chrome. A newly installed app may show notifications silently and without a pop-up. Then open Settings → Apps → *the shop app* → Notifications, allow **sound, vibration and pop-up**, and switch notifications off and on again at the desk.

On an iPhone this only works when the shop is added to the home screen (Share → "Add to Home Screen") and opened from there. Delivery is not guaranteed: the tone at the desk and the callback after three minutes stay.

**Switching off:** tapping the green button **Notifications on** switches them off for this device again; signing out switches them off too. Each device has its own switch: you switch your phone and your computer on and off separately.

## Holding a conversation

- **Lists:** waiting conversations (oldest first, with waiting time), yours, and other open ones.
- **Take:** takes a conversation. Any answer also takes a waiting one. The customer sees who is advising them.
- **Answering:** **Enter** sends the message, **Shift+Enter** (or **Ctrl+Enter**) makes a new line. On a phone Enter makes a new line, and **Send** sends.
- **Share:** recommend a product (a card with image and price), share a link, or send a file from the media library or from your own computer (images, PDF, Word, Excel, LibreOffice, AutoCAD, up to 20 MB).
- **End:** closes the conversation. The customer can then rate the advice with 1 to 5 stars.

If the customer has left the shop, your answer reaches them by email after about a minute, with a link back into the chat.

## Calling

In a conversation you have taken, **Call** (audio only) or **Video call** (with your camera) calls the customer right in the shop; it rings in their chat. If the customer asks for a call, "Call requested" appears in the conversation and you hear a tone. Customers can also start a conversation that way, without a message: such conversations are marked **Call** in the list and show "Would like to talk: please call." until you call. During the call, microphone, camera and screen sharing are **green** while they are on; a muted microphone is **red**.

- **The call card** at the bottom right shows the call. It stays while you look at other conversations; **To the conversation** leads back. You are in one call at a time.
- **During the call:** microphone and camera on and off, **Share screen** (e.g. a plan or the configurator; if the other side shares, your share ends), **Full screen**, and under the sliders icon the choice of microphone and camera. A green ring shows who is speaking.
- **Hang up** ends the call. The conversation then shows how it went, e.g. "Video call, 14:05–14:20", "Call declined" or "Missed call" (after 45 seconds without an answer).

The browser asks for microphone and camera at the first call. Calls are not recorded. If a connection cannot be made, the conversation shows "The call could not be connected.", and *Incidents* shows why.

## What you see about the customer

Next to the conversation (below it on narrow screens):

- a green dot while they are in the shop, and **Currently on**: the page they are looking at;
- their basket and latest orders;
- their **Offers**, with **New offer** (signed-in customers only, see *Offers*);
- **Earlier conversations** with date, consultant and rating.

## Conversation archive

**Archive** at the top of the desk opens the **conversation archive**: all closed conversations, newest first, searchable by name, email or subject, optionally **Only my conversations**. Closed conversations are read-only.
