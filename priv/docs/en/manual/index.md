The back office is divided into areas. You only see the areas your roles allow.

| Area | For | Roles |
|---|---|---|
| **Sales** | Orders, advice (conversations), offers, contact requests | Sales, sales management |
| **Catalogue** | Categories, products with variants, prices, images and configurator | Product management |
| **Content** | Pages, media, terms and conditions | Content management |
| **Administration** | Users, tax classes, payment providers, incidents | Administrators (incidents also sales) |

The **?** at the top of a page opens the matching chapter of this help.
