## My suggestion: offer the three ways side by side, so customers pick what suits them

**1. A new CMS block, "Kontaktwege", with three equal cards** (for the start page, and also usable on the contact page and in help):

```

│  ● 2 Holzexperten sind gerade für Sie da                       │

└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Live availability:** `Presence.available_count()` already exists. When nobody is at the desk, the line and buttons change ("Gerade niemand erreichbar – wir melden uns"), and the call card offers a callback, which is already built (#95).
- **"Video only if you want":** this reassures people who are shy about calling. They don't have to show their face.

**2. Start with a call, without writing first.** This is the actual new feature. "Anrufen" would start a conversation with an automatic line such as "Kunde möchte telefonieren" plus a call request. **3. A start screen in the chat widget.** When there is no conversation yet, the widget would show the same three choices instead of only a text box. The floating button and "Beraten lassen" would both lead there. Once a conversation is running, the call button in the header gets a text label ("Lieber sprechen?"), not just an icon, and it would also show while the customer is still waiting.

**4. Optional: a classic phone number** from the brand config as a fourth way, for people who don't trust calling from the browser.
